Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

This policy explains what information The Toddler Program ("we", "us") collects through thetoddlerprogram.org, how we use it, and the choices you have. We are a small preschool in Kaimukī, Honolulu.

Information we collect

  • Applications. When a parent or legal guardian completes our online application, we collect the information on our application form: the child's name, birth date and gender; home address and phone; each parent's or guardian's name, phone numbers, email, occupation and employer; family questions (siblings, who the child lives with, how you heard about us, kindergarten plans, volunteer interest); health information you choose to share (special health needs, allergies, plan for care when ill); daily routine information (sleep, naps, toileting); a description of your child's personality; emergency contacts; your answers to our parent questionnaire; and your fee payment choice or fee waiver request.
  • Tour requests. Your name, email, phone, your child's age, your preferred date and time, and any notes.
  • When you contact us by phone or email, the information you choose to give us.
  • Communication preferences, if you opt in to school news, event updates or text messages.
  • Website usage data collected by analytics and advertising tools (see "Cookies, analytics and advertising" below). We do not put names, emails, phone numbers or form answers into these tools.

How we use information

We use application and tour information only to process admissions, schedule and confirm tours, contact you about your application, and, for enrolled children, care for your child. If you opt in, we also use your contact details to send school news and event updates. We use website usage data to understand how the site is used and to measure our own outreach.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with other organizations for their own marketing.

How information is stored and who can access it

Online submissions are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection and stored in The Toddler Program's Google Workspace account (Google Sheets). Access is limited to authorized school staff who need it for admissions and enrollment. Google acts as our service provider for this storage. We may use service providers, such as email or customer relationship tools, that receive only contact details and are bound to protect them.

How long we keep information

Records for enrolled children are kept as required for school records. Applications for children who do not enroll are deleted within 2 years of the application date unless you ask us to delete them sooner. Tour requests are kept for no longer than the admissions cycle they relate to.

Children's privacy

Our website is intended for parents and guardians. Information about a child is provided by the child's parent or legal guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children, and children should not use our forms. Our practices are designed to align with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). If you believe a child has submitted information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use our site (for example, pages viewed and clicks on "Book a Tour", phone and email links). We have turned off Google signals and ad personalization for this data. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

We use the Meta Pixel to measure the results of our Facebook and Instagram outreach (for example, page views, tour requests and applications). Meta may use cookies and receive information about your visit, such as your device and the pages you view. We do not send names, emails, phone numbers or form answers to Meta. You can manage how Meta uses this information in your Meta ad preferences, and learn more at Meta's cookies policy.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of interest-based ads from many companies at optout.aboutads.info. Our site does not currently respond to "Do Not Track" signals.

Payments

You may pay the application fee by check payable to The Toddler Program. If online payment is enabled, card payments are processed by Stripe; we do not receive or store your full card number.

Email and text messages

We will only send school news and event updates if you check the box to receive them, and only send text messages if you separately opt in to texts. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop texts at any time and HELP for help, or contact us. You can unsubscribe from emails using the link in any email or by contacting us. Consent to marketing messages is never a condition of applying. We do not share your phone number or text-messaging consent with third parties for their marketing.

Your choices and rights

Parents and guardians may ask to access, correct or delete the information they submitted, or withdraw consent to marketing messages, by contacting us below. We may need to keep certain information for enrolled children where the law or licensing requires it, and we will verify your identity before acting on a request.

California and other state privacy rights

Depending on where you live, state law (for example in California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and other states) may give you rights to know what personal information we hold, to request deletion or correction, and to opt out of the sale of personal information or its use for targeted advertising. Some state privacy laws may not apply to us, but we will honor reasonable requests to access, correct or delete your information. We do not sell personal information. California residents may also request information about disclosures for direct marketing purposes; we do not share personal information with third parties for their direct marketing.

Security and data breaches

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, such as encrypted connections and restricted account access, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If a security breach involving your personal information occurs, we will notify affected individuals as required by Hawaii law (Hawaii Revised Statutes Chapter 487N) and any other applicable law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it last changed. Significant changes will be posted on this page.
